Description

Our Music Library is a third-party music player for your own self-hosted Navidrome or Subsonic (OpenSubsonic) server. It requires a server you already run and includes no music of its own — Our Music Library simply plays the library you already have, and makes it feel at home on your iPhone and iPad. BUILT FOR THE WHOLE FAMILY - Give each person their own profile. Everyone sees their own library plus a shared library the whole household can enjoy. - On a shared iPhone or iPad, an optional profile picker lets whoever picked up the device choose who's listening. - A parent area, protected by Face ID or a passcode, keeps server settings out of little hands. YOUR LIBRARY, ORGANIZED - Browse by Artists, Albums, Genres, and Recently Added. - Open any album to see its full track list and artwork. - Tap a track and it plays. A PLAYER YOU'LL ENJOY - A full-screen player with a play queue, shuffle, and repeat. - A mini-player stays with you while you keep browsing. - Album artwork throughout. PLAYS THE WAY YOU LISTEN - Streams over your own network. - Keeps playing in the background. - Full Lock Screen and Control Center controls. - AirPlay to your speakers, Apple TV, and other devices. PRIVATE BY DESIGN - Stores only your server login, kept in the iOS Keychain. - No ads. No tracking. No analytics. No accounts to create. - Your music and your server stay yours. WHAT YOU'LL NEED Our Music Library connects to a Navidrome or Subsonic (OpenSubsonic) server that you host yourself. If you don't already run one, this app won't have anything to play. Point it at your server, sign in, and your library is ready. Our Music Library is an independent app and is not affiliated with the Navidrome or Subsonic projects.
