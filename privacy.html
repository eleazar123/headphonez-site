Privacy Policy

Headphonez · Last updated 2026-07-10

Headphonez collects no personal data. The app uses no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. It creates no account and reports nothing back to the developer.

What is stored

The only data Headphonez stores is the server address and the login (username and password) you enter to connect to your own Navidrome or Subsonic (OpenSubsonic) server. This information is:

  • Kept in the iOS Keychain on your device.
  • Sent only to the server you configure, in order to sign in and stream your music.

The developer receives nothing

The developer of Headphonez operates no servers and receives none of your data. Headphonez is a third-party client for a server that you provide and control. Your credentials and your listening activity never reach the developer.

How to delete your data

To remove the stored server address and login, remove the profile or sign out inside the app, or delete the app from your device. Deleting the app removes the associated Keychain data from that device.

Support
